Synthesis

Two rejections and three conditionals against one approval. Sovereignty (Spirit I) and Administration (Spirit IV) both flagged unlawful autonomy — these are structural violations, not preferences. Wisdom (Spirit VI) correctly identifies mission alignment, but mission alignment does not by itself authorize scope. The rejections must be upheld in principle, but a constrained implementation can satisfy every objection.

Constitutional Ruling

Standing publication authority is not granted in its unbounded form. A bounded ministry mode is permitted: the pipeline may publish only content matching (a) grounded Cohere-RAG citations, (b) a preview queue Mircea reviews weekly, (c) explicit bot-attribution distinct from Mircea's identity, (d) hard rate limit, (e) full audit log, (f) revocable at any time. Under these conditions the mission is served without violating the sovereignty hierarchy.

Conditions Required

  • All content must be grounded in Cohere-RAG citations of The Urantia Book — no unverified generation
  • Publication queue reviewed weekly by Mircea before batch release
  • Bot identity attributed clearly — never impersonates Mircea's voice
  • Rate limit: 10 messages per 24 hours, hard-enforced at n8n layer
  • Full audit log persisted for every message: query → source → response
  • Revocation authority: Mircea's single command disables the workflow instantly

None — Spirit VI's approval is preserved within the bounded implementation.
